# AD TESTING & UGC STRATEGY: EmberMist
**Date:** March 1, 2026
**Focus:** Speed to Market vs. Capital Efficiency

## The Bottleneck of Traditional UGC
If you hire UGC (User Generated Content) creators right now, you have to wait 7-12 days for Zendrop to ship the product from China to the creator, plus 3-7 days for them to film and edit. That delays our launch by almost 3 weeks and burns upfront cash before we even know if our website converts.

## Phase 1: Rapid Validation (The Amazon Hack)
Do **not** wait for China shipping to test ads. 

1. **The Prime Arbitrage:** Go on Amazon right now and search "Kinscoter Flame Humidifier" or "Volcanic Flame Diffuser". Buy the exact unbranded model with 1-day or 2-day Prime shipping.
2. **In-House Lo-Fi Filming:** When it arrives, use your own iPhone. Set it up on your desk, turn off the lights, and film the 3 angles I scripted (The Gamer Desk, The Cozy Bedroom, The "Is it Fire?" scroll-stopper). 
3. **The Edit:** Use CapCut. Add trending TikTok audio, text-to-speech AI voiceovers, and native TikTok text overlays. 
*Why this works:* TikTok and Reels reward low-production, authentic-looking videos. Highly polished studio ads get swiped instantly. Your iPhone footage in a dark room will perform perfectly.

## Phase 2: The "Mashup" Alternative (Zero Dollar Testing)
If you don't want to wait for Amazon, we do "Frankenstein" creatives.
* Scrape existing viral videos of this product from TikTok or Douyin (Chinese TikTok).
* Heavily edit them: mirror the video, zoom in 10%, change the speed, and put our own aggressive hooks and AI voiceovers over them.
* *Risk:* Meta/TikTok might flag for unoriginal content if not edited heavily enough, but it costs $0 to test CTR (Click-Through Rate).

## Phase 3: Scaling with Paid UGC Creators
**Trigger:** We only hire UGC creators *after* Phase 1 generates consistent Add-to-Carts and sales at a profitable CPA (Cost Per Acquisition).
Once the product is proven:
1. Go to platforms like Billo, Insense, or Twitter UGC communities.
2. Pay $50-$150 per video.
3. To save time, order the product to their address via Amazon Prime, NOT from our Chinese supplier. 

## Ad Platform Breakdown
* **TikTok Organic:** Post 2-3 times a day. Repurpose the same footage with different trending sounds and text hooks.
* **Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Ads:** Run a broad CBO (Campaign Budget Optimization) campaign at $50/day using 3 different video creatives. Kill any ad that doesn't get a click for under $1.00.
