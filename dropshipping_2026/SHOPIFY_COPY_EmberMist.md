## 2. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Is the "flame" actually hot? Is it safe around pets and kids?**
Absolutely safe. The "flame" is a brilliant optical illusion created by LED lights reflecting off the cool ultrasonic water mist. It is completely safe to touch and perfect for homes with children or pets.

**Can I use my own essential oils?**
Yes! Just add 2-3 drops of your favorite water-soluble essential oils or fragrances directly into the water tank to enjoy hours of aromatherapy.

**Does it leave condensation on my desk?**
No. The EmberMist uses high-frequency ultrasonic technology to atomize water into an ultra-fine mist that evaporates into the air rather than pooling on your surfaces.

**How long does a full tank last?**
A single fill of the 130ml tank provides up to 6-9 hours of continuous mist and fragrance.

**How long will it take to receive my order?**
Orders are processed within 1-3 business days. Due to high demand, standard shipping typically takes between 7 to 14 business days. You will receive a tracking number as soon as your order ships.
