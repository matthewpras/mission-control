# EXECUTIVE OVERRIDE: Pivot from AI Skincare Monitor
**Date:** March 1, 2026
**Initiated By:** Matthew (Director)
**Reason:** Unacceptable operational risk profile (chargebacks, customer support overhead, app dependency).

## Revised Strategy: The "Dumb" High-Margin Sweet Spot
We are pivoting to **Option 3: Aesthetic Desk Purifiers / Humidifiers**.

### Why This Works (The New Math):
* **Sourcing:** ~$10 - $12
* **Retail Target:** $49.99
* **Gross Profit:** ~$38 per unit
* **Risk Profile:** LOW. It is a "dumb" electronic (plug-and-play USB power, water tank, LED lights). No companion apps, no bluetooth pairing issues, virtually zero software-related chargebacks.
* **Marketability:** Extremely high visual appeal for "desk setup", "gaming room", or "car aesthetic" videos on TikTok and Instagram Reels. We can use influencer seeding and mid-tier Meta ads.

### Action Plan
1.  **Brand Identity:** Clean, minimalist, wellness-meets-tech vibe.
2.  **Store Schema:** Single-product focus with heavy emphasis on GIF demonstrations and lifestyle photography.
3.  **Upsells:** Essential oil drops (massive margin booster, recurring revenue potential).
