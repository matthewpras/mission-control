# EmberMist Shopify Theme & Layout Guide

## 1. Global Brand Settings

### Color Palette
*   **Primary Background:** Matte Black (`#1A1A1A`) - Used for the header, footer, and striking alternating sections to give a premium, sleek feel.
*   **Secondary Background:** Off-White/Light Gray (`#F8F9FA`) - Used for text-heavy sections to maintain readability.
*   **Accent / Call-to-Action (CTA):** Volcanic Flame (`#FF4500`) - Used EXCLUSIVELY for "Add to Cart", "Checkout", and promotional banners to drive conversions.
*   **Text (Dark Theme):** White (`#FFFFFF`) or Light Gray (`#E0E0E0`).
*   **Text (Light Theme):** Deep Charcoal (`#212529`).

### Typography
*   **Headings:** Montserrat or Poppins (Bold, sans-serif, modern, impactful).
*   **Body Copy:** Inter or Roboto (Clean, highly legible for mobile).

## 2. Homepage Layout

**Section 1: Hero Banner (Above the Fold)**
*   **Visual:** High-quality looping background video (or high-res GIF) of the EmberMist diffuser erupting in a dimly lit room.
*   **Headline:** Transform Your Space. Elevate Your Senses.
*   **Subheadline:** The ultra-realistic volcanic flame diffuser that brings calm and elegance to any room.
*   **CTA Button:** `Shop Now` (Color: `#FF4500`) linking directly to the product page.

**Section 2: Trust Badges (Social Proof)**
*   Row of logos: "Free Shipping", "30-Day Guarantee", "10,000+ Happy Customers", "Safe & Silent".

**Section 3: Feature Highlights (Image + Text Alternating)**
*   *Block A:* Image of diffuser on a nightstand. Text: "Whisper-Quiet Relaxation."
*   *Block B:* Image showing essential oils being added. Text: "Aromatherapy Ready."
*   *Block C:* Close-up of the smart chip/water tank. Text: "Smart Auto Shut-Off."

**Section 4: User Generated Content / Testimonials**
*   Carousel of 3-4 photo reviews showing the diffuser in real customers' homes.
*   Star ratings (`#FF4500` for the stars).

**Section 5: Footer**
*   Links: Contact Us, Track Order, Shipping Policy, Refund Policy, Privacy Policy.
*   Newsletter signup.

## 3. Product Page Layout (High Conversion)

**Media Gallery (Left/Top)**
*   Main image: High-res lifestyle shot.
*   2-3 short GIFs demonstrating the "Flame" and "Volcano" modes.
*   Infographic image highlighting dimensions and tank capacity (360ml).

**Buy Box (Right/Below Media)**
*   **Product Title:** EmberMist Volcanic Flame Diffuser.
*   **Price:** Highlight sale price (e.g., $39.99 crossed out from $69.99).
*   **Reviews:** 5-star aggregate badge (e.g., "⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (245 Reviews)").
*   **Variant Selector:** Black / White (if applicable).
*   **Add to Cart:** Large, sticky, pulsating or solid `#FF4500` button.
*   **Trust text below CTA:** "🔒 Secure Checkout | 🚚 Fast Shipping"

**Product Description (Below Buy Box)**
*   Tabbed or Accordion layout:
    *   **Description:** (Use the generated EmberMist copy).
    *   **How to Use:** Simple 3-step icon guide (Fill Water -> Add Drops -> Turn On).
    *   **Shipping & Returns:** (Use generated policy).
    *   **FAQ:** (Use generated FAQ).

## 4. Navigation & Header
*   **Style:** Transparent on Hero section, sticky solid black (`#1A1A1A`) on scroll.
*   **Links:** Home | Shop | Track Order | FAQ.
*   **Cart Icon:** Slide-out drawer cart to keep users on the page, displaying a progress bar for "Free Shipping unlocks at $50".
