## FAQ
**Q: Is the flame real?**
A: No, it is a safe, cold mist illuminated by smart LEDs to simulate a realistic flame.

**Q: Can I use essential oils?**
A: Yes, simply add a few drops of your preferred water-soluble essential oil to the water tank.

**Q: How long does it run on a full tank?**
A: The EmberMist diffuses continuously for 6-9 hours depending on the mist setting.
