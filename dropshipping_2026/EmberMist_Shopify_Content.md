## FAQ
**Q: Is the flame safe to touch?**
A: Yes! The "flame" is actually a cool mist illuminated by LED lights. It is completely safe to touch and won't burn you.

**Q: Can I use any essential oil with EmberMist?**
A: Yes, you can use any water-soluble essential oil. Just add 2-3 drops into the water tank.

**Q: How long does the water tank last?**
A: The 360ml tank provides up to 8-10 hours of continuous misting on a single fill.

**Q: Does it have an automatic shut-off feature?**
A: Absolutely. EmberMist automatically powers down when the water level is too low, preventing any damage to the unit.
