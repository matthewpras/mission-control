# EMBERMIST: Shopify Store Schema & Build Guide
**Date:** March 1, 2026
**Objective:** A high-converting, single-product-focused, mobile-first Shopify store that looks like a premium tech/wellness brand, not a dropshipping site.

## 1. Theme & Branding
**Theme:** *Sense* or *Dawn* (Free Shopify 2.0 Themes). They are lightning-fast and highly customizable for single-product funnels.
**Typography:** 
*   Headings: *Inter* or *Montserrat* (Clean, modern tech).
*   Body text: *Roboto* or *System-ui* (Highly readable on mobile).

**Color Palette (Custom Hex Codes from Logo):**
*   **Background (Dark Mode Vibe):** `#1A1A1A` (Dark Charcoal/Black - provides a sleek, modern canvas).
*   **Surface/Cards:** A slightly lighter charcoal or pure `#1A1A1A` with subtle borders.
*   **Primary Accent / Buy Buttons:** `#FF4500` (Vibrant Orange/Red - high-conversion CTA color).
*   **Secondary Accent / Highlights:** `#FFA500` (Warm Amber - for hover states or sale badges) or `#8B0000` (Deep Red - for urgency/sale text).
*   **Text:** `#F2F2F2` (Off-White - ensures perfect readability against the dark background without glaring like pure white).

## 2. Store Architecture (The Funnel)

### The Homepage / Landing Page (Mobile First)
*   **Header:** Minimalist logo ("EmberMist"). Sticky header enabled.
*   **Hero Section:** 
    *   **Background:** A high-quality looping GIF (not a static image) of the humidifier running in a dark, aesthetic room. 
    *   **Headline:** "The Atmosphere You Deserve."
    *   **Subheadline:** "Upgrade your space with the mesmerizing illusion of fire. Whisper-quiet aromatherapy for your desk or bedroom."
    *   **CTA Button:** "Shop Now - 40% Off" (Directs to product page).
*   **Social Proof Bar:** "As seen on TikTok" / "Over 10,000+ Cozy Spaces Upgraded."
*   **Benefit Icons (3 Columns):** 💧 Hydrates Air | 🧘‍♀️ Reduces Stress | 🔥 Safe Cold-Mist Tech.

### The Product Page (PDP) - Where the Money is Made
*   **Media Gallery:** 
    *   Image 1: Clean product shot (Black model) on a dark background.
    *   Image 2: GIF of someone putting their hand through the mist (Safety proof).
    *   Image 3: GIF of essential oils dropping in (Aromatherapy proof).
    *   Image 4: Lifestyle shot on a gaming desk.
*   **Sticky Add to Cart:** A button that stays at the bottom of the screen as they scroll on mobile.
*   **Pricing:** `$49.99` (Crossed out `$85.00`).
*   **Shipping Text:** "🚚 Ships in 24 Hours | Delivered in 7-12 Days" (Be honest to avoid chargebacks, frame it nicely).
*   **Trust Badges:** SSL Checkout, 30-Day Guarantee, Safe/Cold Mist Certified.

## 3. The App Stack (To Push AOV over $50)
Keep the app count low so the site loads instantly.

1.  **Loox or AliReviews (Social Proof):** Import 15-20 photo reviews from AliExpress. Filter for ONLY 5-star reviews with photos of the black model in dark rooms. Change the names to English/US names.
2.  **Kaching Bundle Quantity Breaks (AOV Booster):** 
    *   "Buy 1: $49.99"
    *   "Buy 2 (Gift a Friend): $85.00 (Save 15%)"
    *   "Buy 3 (Whole Home Setup): $115.00 (Save 25%)"
3.  **Klaviyo or SMSBump (Retention):** A pop-up offering "10% Off Your First Order" to capture emails. Since it's an impulse buy, we need to retarget people who abandon their cart.
