## 3. Shipping & Logistics Strategy
To ensure fast delivery and avoid chargebacks, we MUST adhere to the following logistics constraints:

*   **Rule #1: NO BUILT-IN BATTERIES.** We will ONLY source the **USB Type-C plug-in** models. Devices with built-in lithium-ion batteries require special "Dangerous Goods" shipping lines. This adds 5-10 days to delivery times and increases customs rejection risks. Plug-in USB models ship via standard fast-track lines.
*   **Rule #2: The 7-12 Day Shipping Window.** We will not use standard AliExpress ePacket (which takes 15-30 days). We will use **YunExpress** (via a Private Agent) or **Zendrop Express**. Both average 7-12 days to the US mainland with full end-to-end USPS tracking.
*   **Rule #3: Box Protection.** The product is mostly plastic casing. We must explicitly instruct the supplier (or ensure Zendrop) uses bubble-wrap or corner-protected boxes to prevent transit crushing, keeping the perceived premium value intact upon unboxing.

## 4. The Upsell Logistics (Essential Oils)
*   Do NOT dropship liquids (Essential Oils) from China. Shipping liquids across borders triggers intense customs inspections, delaying the main product.
*   **Solution:** We will either source an essential oil supplier based natively in the USA (like via Spocket/Zendrop US warehouses) or simply sell the humidifier as a "use with any of your favorite scents" standalone device to keep logistics frictionless.

## Conclusion
The logistics profile is extremely favorable, provided we source the **USB-powered (non-battery)** variant. We can reliably hit 7-12 day shipping times with a total landed cost under $17, allowing us to maintain almost a $35+ margin per sale.
