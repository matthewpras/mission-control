# EMBERMIST: iPhone Ad Shooting Guide
**Date:** March 1, 2026
**Equipment Needed:** iPhone, EmberMist Unit, Water, Essential Oil (any kind, for the shot), a dark room.

## 📱 Camera Settings (Crucial)
1. **Format:** 4K at 60 FPS (Settings > Camera > Record Video). This makes the mist look incredibly smooth and premium.
2. **Exposure Lock:** Tap and hold on the "flame" until you see **AE/AF LOCK** pop up. Then, drag the sun icon *down* slightly to make the room darker and the LED flame pop harder.
3. **Wipe your lens:** Seriously, wipe the camera lens with your shirt. Smudges ruin the crisp aesthetic.

## 🎬 Shot List & Angles

### Angle 1: The "Gamer / WFH Desk Setup"
**Vibe:** Dark, moody, tech-focused.
**The Setup:** Place the unit under a monitor or next to a keyboard. Room lights OFF. Monitor screen ON (displaying a cool wallpaper or code). 
**The Shots (Film 5-10 seconds each):**
*   **Shot 1.1 (The Hook):** Hand plugs in the USB-C cable. The flame instantly turns on.
*   **Shot 1.2 (The Pan):** Start close on the flame, slowly pan the camera back to reveal the whole desk setup looking elevated and moody.
*   **Shot 1.3 (The Detail):** Extreme close-up of the mist rolling out of the top slit.

### Angle 2: The "Cozy / Wellness Bedroom"
**Vibe:** Warm, relaxing, aesthetic room decor.
**The Setup:** Place on a nightstand or dresser. Dim warm ambient lighting (like a small lamp in the background).
**The Shots:**
*   **Shot 2.1 (The Drop):** Hold a small bottle of essential oil. Squeeze exactly 2 drops into the water tank (top off). Make sure the camera focuses on the drops hitting the water.
*   **Shot 2.2 (The Illusion):** Film from a low angle looking slightly up at the device so the mist looks exactly like a miniature fireplace.
*   **Shot 2.3 (The Vibe):** A wider shot showing the device glowing next to a neatly made bed or a stack of books.

### Angle 3: The "Scroll-Stopper" (Is it fire?)
**Vibe:** Fast-paced, disruptive, TikTok-native.
**The Setup:** Anywhere dark.
**The Shots:**
*   **Shot 3.1 (The Hand Test):** Plunge your hand directly through the "flame". Wiggle your fingers in the mist to prove it's cold and safe. This is your #1 hook.
*   **Shot 3.2 (The Paper Test):** Hold a piece of paper right above the flame. Pull it away to show it's completely unburned, just slightly damp. (High engagement bait).

## ✂️ Editing Notes (CapCut)
*   Keep clips between 1.5 to 3 seconds max. Fast cuts retain attention.
*   We will overlay the AI Voiceovers and trending sounds during the edit phase. Just focus on getting raw, stable footage.
