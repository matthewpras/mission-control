# Kalshi Bot: Experimental Volatility Strategy (V1)

## Focus Markets (Paper Trading Only)
- **Sports & Entertainment:** NBA/NHL Props, Premier League, Oscars.
- **Crypto:** BTC/ETH Strike Prices (Daily/Weekly).
- **Earnings:** Tech Earnings reports.

## Objective
The goal is not steady state mean-reversion, but *variance harvesting* and *event-driven pricing models*. We are paper trading these to identify if Kalshi's user base systematically misprices long-tail outcomes or overreacts to momentum.

## The Strategy Phase
1. **Data Gathering:** Buy small, highly volatile positions (10-25c range) where a single event drastically shifts the probability.
2. **Analysis:** Review whether the bot can exit on the spike (momentum) or if it holds to binary resolution.
3. **Outcome:** Identify true alpha for our Level 2 and Level 3 growth paths before risking real capital.
