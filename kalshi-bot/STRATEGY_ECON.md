# Kalshi Bot: Economic Stats Strategy (V1)

## Focus Markets
- **Federal Funds Rate:** (FED-YYMMM-TXX)
- **CPI/Inflation:** (CPI-YYMMM)
- **Non-Farm Payrolls (Employment):** (NFP-YYMMM)

## The "Steady State" Algorithm
1. **Mean Reversion:** Economic stats markets often overreact to "whisper numbers" or breaking news headlines before the actual data drops. 
2. **Strategy:** Identify probability spikes that deviate from the 24-hour moving average by >15% without a corresponding data release.
3. **Paper Trading Phase:**
   - Log the "Market Price" at entry.
   - Calculate theoretical "Profit" based on a $100 hypothetical position.
   - Hold until either (a) Target 10% gain reached or (b) Official data release.

## Growth Path
- **Level 1 (Current):** Economic Stats (Low volatility, high predictability).
- **Level 2 (Future):** Tech Earnings / Political Events (Medium volatility).
- **Level 3 (Future):** BTC/ETH Strike Prices (High volatility, requires faster execution).
