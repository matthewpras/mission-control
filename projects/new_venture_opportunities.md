# New Venture Opportunities (2026)

Based on our criteria for high-margin, non-arbitrage, non-dropshipping online businesses, here are three emerging models:

1. **AI-First Vertical Micro-SaaS:**
   - **The Gap:** Broad AI tools are commoditized. The gap is in hyper-specific workflows for niche industries (legal, specialized healthcare, hyper-local logistics).
   - **Margin Profile:** While AI inference costs keep broad AI SaaS margins around 50-65%, vertical micro-SaaS can charge a premium for workflow integration, lifting effective margins.
   - **Execution:** Build a single-feature wrapper around an LLM API that completely automates one tedious industry-specific task.

2. **B2B API Integrations / "Glue" Services:**
   - **The Gap:** Companies have adopted AI but lack the interconnected infrastructure to make it useful across their existing software stack (the "intelligent interconnected business model" trend for 2026).
   - **Margin Profile:** Extremely high (80%+). Maintenance is low once the API connector is stable.
   - **Execution:** Build standardized, secure connectors that link legacy enterprise tools to modern AI endpoints.

3. **Data Curation & Licensing:**
   - **The Gap:** AI companies are starved for high-quality, domain-specific training data. Public data is exhausted or legally risky.
   - **Margin Profile:** High initial labor/scraping cost, near 100% margin on resale/licensing.
   - **Execution:** Systematically collect, clean, and structure niche datasets (e.g., specialized manufacturing specs, historical local market data) and license them to AI developers or hedge funds via a subscription API.
